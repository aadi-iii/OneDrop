FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before you donate or search.

Who can donate blood?

Most healthy adults aged 18–65, weighing over 50kg, can donate blood every 90 days. A quick screening confirms your eligibility before every donation.

Is donating blood safe?

Yes. All equipment used is sterile and single-use. The process is supervised by trained medical staff and takes about 10–15 minutes.

How quickly can I find blood in an emergency?

OneDrop instantly notifies compatible, nearby donors and partner blood banks the moment an emergency request is raised.

Is my personal information kept private?

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted and only shared with matched donors or recipients with your explicit consent.

How often can I donate?

Whole blood donation is generally recommended every 90 days to allow your body to fully replenish.
